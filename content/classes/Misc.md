## Data 100 (Spring 25)
I appreciated this course for teaching me practical skills with pandas and SQL. On the conceptual side, 16A and 70 covered much of what Data 100 taught, except in a more rigourous manner.

In general, Data 100 is a well-designed, practical course quite useful for industry. It taught important data science concepts like cross-fold validation, least squares, regularization, overfitting/underfitting, and more. These are practical things that I use every day in research or in internships.
