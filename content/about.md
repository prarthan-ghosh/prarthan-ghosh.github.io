Hi, I'm Prarthan! I'm an undergrad at UC Berkeley majoring in Applied Math. My work and interests are primarily at the intersection of computational systems, machine learning, and neuroscience.

Currently, I'm an Engineering Associate at [Pivotal Aero](http://pivotal.aero/) -- I first joined as a Data & ML intern in summer 2025 -- contributing to projects involving camera optics and AWS infrastructure. Since the summer of 2024, I have also been an RA in the [Neural Systems and Machine Learning Lab](https://bouchardlab.lbl.gov/) at LBL, where I investigate dimensionality reduction from both information theoretic and control theoretic perspectives. Previously, I spent three years (2022–2025) as a Research Assistant in the [Ben-Shalom Lab](http://benshalomlab.com/), where I assisted in architecting a deep learning pipeline designed for fitting single-cell neuron models.
